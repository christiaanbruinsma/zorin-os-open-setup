# Office Work: Recommended Applications

List of applications for a complete office work setup on Zorin OS. See [`../../apps/`](../../apps/) for detailed information about each application.

## Core Applications

### Office Suite
- **[OnlyOffice](../../apps/productivity/OnlyOffice.md)** – Modern office suite for creating and editing text documents, spreadsheets, and presentations

### Email & Calendar
- **[Evolution](../../apps/productivity/Evolution.md)** – Full-featured email client with calendar and contacts integration
- **[Thunderbird](../../apps/productivity/Thunderbird.md)** – Open-source email client with calendar support
- **[Calendar](../../apps/productivity/Calendar.md)** – Default GNOME calendar application for managing events and tasks
- **[Contacts](../../apps/productivity/Contacts.md)** – Default GNOME contacts manager for organizing your address book

## Productivity Suite

### Document Creation & Editing
- **[OnlyOffice](../../apps/productivity/OnlyOffice.md)** – Create and edit Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and PowerPoint presentations
- **[Apostrophe](../../apps/productivity/Apostrophe.md)** – Distraction-free Markdown editor for note-taking and writing
- **[Obsidian](../../apps/productivity/Obsidian.md)** – Knowledge base and note-taking application with linking capabilities

## Communication

### Messaging & Collaboration
- **[Telegram](../../apps/productivity/Telegram.md)** – Cloud-based messaging app for teams and individuals
- **[Signal Desktop](../../apps/productivity/Signal Desktop.md)** – Private messaging with end-to-end encryption
- **[Ferdium](../../apps/productivity/Ferdium.md)** – Multi-messenger client for combining multiple chat services in one app
- **[Zoom](../../apps/productivity/Zoom.md)** – Video conferencing for meetings and webinars

## Organization

### Utilities for Office Work
- **[Passwords and Keys](../../apps/utilities/Passwords and Keys.md)** – Securely store passwords and manage encryption keys
- **[Proton Pass](../../apps/utilities/Proton Pass.md)** – Alternative password manager with end-to-end encryption
- **[Fonts](../../apps/utilities/Fonts.md)** – Manage and preview fonts on your system
- **[Text Editor](../../apps/utilities/Text Editor.md)** – Simple text editor for quick edits

## Optional Add-ons

- **[Google Chrome](../../apps/utilities/Google Chrome.md)** – For users who need Google Chrome compatibility
- **[Chromium](../../apps/utilities/Chromium.md)** – Open-source alternative to Chrome
- **[Firefox](../../apps/utilities/Firefox.md)** – Privacy-focused web browser
- **[Brave Browser](../../apps/utilities/Brave Browser.md)** – Browser with built-in ad blocking and privacy features
