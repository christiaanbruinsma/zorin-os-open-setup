# Creative Work Use Case

A complete setup for digital artists, designers, and content creators on Zorin OS. This use case covers applications for graphic design, digital painting, video editing, audio production, and e-book reading.

## Who This Is For

- Graphic designers and illustrators
- Digital artists and painters
- Video editors and content creators
- Audio producers and musicians
- Writers and e-book readers

## Overview

This use case provides a **privacy-conscious** creative setup with professional-grade tools for various creative disciplines. All applications are chosen for their capabilities, ease of use, and integration with the Zorin OS/GNOME ecosystem.

- **[Setup Instructions](./setup.md)** – Configuration tips and best practices for creative workflows
