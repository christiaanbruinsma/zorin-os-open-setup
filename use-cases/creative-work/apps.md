# Creative Work: Recommended Applications

List of applications for a complete creative work setup on Zorin OS. See [`../../apps/`](../../apps/) for detailed information about each application.

## Graphic Design & Illustration

- **[GIMP](../../apps/creativity/GIMP.md)** – Professional image editing and manipulation (alternative to Photoshop)
- **[Inkscape](../../apps/creativity/Inkscape.md)** – Vector graphics editor for illustrations, logos, and diagrams
- **[Krita](../../apps/creativity/Krita.md)** – Professional digital painting program with advanced brush engines

## Digital Painting & Drawing

- **[Krita](../../apps/creativity/Krita.md)** – Primary digital painting application with 100+ brushes and stabilizers
- **[GIMP](../../apps/creativity/GIMP.md)** – For photo manipulation and graphic design tasks

## Video Editing & Production

- **[Kdenlive](../../apps/creativity/Kdenlive.md)** – Non-linear video editor with multi-track support
- **[HandBrake](../../apps/creativity/HandBrake.md)** – Video transcoder for converting between formats
- **[VLC](../../apps/creativity/VLC.md)** – Media player for previewing video and audio files

## Audio Production

- **[Audacity](../../apps/creativity/Audacity.md)** – Audio editor and recorder for podcasts and music production
- **[VLC](../../apps/creativity/VLC.md)** – Can also play and convert audio files

## E-Book Reading

- **[Foliate](../../apps/creativity/Foliate.md)** – Modern e-book reader with support for EPUB, MOBI, PDF, and more

## Media Management

- **[gThumb](../../apps/creativity/gThumb.md)** – Image viewer and organizer
- **[Image Viewer](../../apps/utilities/Image Viewer.md)** – Default GNOME image viewer for quick previews

## Utilities for Creative Work

- **[Fonts](../../apps/utilities/Fonts.md)** – Manage and preview system fonts for design work
- **[Gradia](../../apps/utilities/Gradia.md)** – Enhance and annotate screenshots for presentations
- **[Halftone](../../apps/utilities/Halftone.md)** – Apply dithering effects to images for pixel art style

## Optional Add-ons

- **[Signal Desktop](../../apps/productivity/Signal Desktop.md)** – For secure communication with clients
- **[Obsidian](../../apps/productivity/Obsidian.md)** – For documenting creative projects and ideas

*For detailed information about each application, see the individual app pages in the [`../../apps/`](../../apps/) directory.*
