# HandBrake

**Category:** Creativity → Video Transcoding
**Website:** [https://handbrake.fr/](https://handbrake.fr/)

Use this app to convert video from nearly any format. HandBrake is an open-source video transcoder that converts video files to modern, widely supported codecs, making it ideal for creating compatible versions of your videos for various devices and platforms.

## Key Features
- Built-in device presets for quick starts
- Supports most common multimedia files and DVD/BluRay sources (without copy protection)
- Output containers: MP4, MKV, WebM
- Video encoders: AV1, H.265 (HEVC), H.264 (AVC), MPEG-4, MPEG-2, VP8, VP9
- Optional hardware-accelerated video encoding
- Audio encoders: AAC, HE-AAC, MP3, FLAC, AC3, E-AC3, Opus, Vorbis
- Audio pass-thru for AC-3, E-AC3, FLAC, DTS, DTS-HD, TrueHD, AAC, Opus, MP3, MP2
- Title, chapter, and range selection
- Batch scan and queueing of encodes
- Chapter markers
- Subtitles support (VobSub, Closed Captions CEA-608, SSA, SRT)
- Constant Quality or Average Bitrate Video Encoding
- Support for Variable Frame Rate (VFR) and Constant Frame Rate (CFR)
- Video filters: Deinterlacing, Decomb, Denoise, Detelecine, Deblock, Grayscale, Colourspace, Cropping, Scaling
- Live static and video preview
- HDR Pass-thru
