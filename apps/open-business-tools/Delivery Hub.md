# Delivery Hub

**Category:** Open Business Tools → Workspace & Delivery
**Website:** https://github.com/christiaanbruinsma/delivery-hub

Prepare and verify delivery packages with manifests, checksums and structured ZIP exports. Ensures accurate and reliable delivery of files to clients or team members.

## Key Features
- Create delivery packages with manifests
- Generate checksums for file verification
- Structured ZIP exports
- Delivery verification tools

## How to Get It
Delivery Hub is available on GitHub. Download the latest release from the project page.

[Download Delivery Hub](https://github.com/christiaanbruinsma/delivery-hub)

## Privacy & Notes
- Local-first application, no account or online service required
- Open source software
- All data remains on your local machine
