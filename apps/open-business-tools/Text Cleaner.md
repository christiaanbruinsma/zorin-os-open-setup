# Text Cleaner

**Category:** Open Business Tools → Everyday Work<br>
**Website:** https://github.com/christiaanbruinsma/text-cleaner

Inspect and clean text copied from websites, AI tools, email and office documents. Remove unwanted formatting and characters.

## Key Features
- Clean text from various sources
- Remove hidden formatting and special characters
- Inspect text before and after cleaning
- Preserve essential content

## How to Get It
Text Cleaner is available on GitHub. Download the latest release from the project page.

[Download Text Cleaner](https://github.com/christiaanbruinsma/text-cleaner)

## Privacy & Notes
- Local-first application, no account or online service required
- Open source software
- All text processing happens on your local machine
