# QR Generator

**Category:** Open Business Tools → Everyday Work
**Website:** https://github.com/christiaanbruinsma/qr-generator

Create and export QR codes locally as PNG or SVG without accounts, tracking or history. Generate QR codes for various use cases while maintaining privacy.

## Key Features
- Create QR codes from text, URLs or other data
- Export as PNG or SVG format
- No accounts or online services required
- No tracking or history stored

## How to Get It
QR Generator is available on GitHub. Download the latest release from the project page.

[Download QR Generator](https://github.com/christiaanbruinsma/qr-generator)

## Privacy & Notes
- Local-first application, no account or online service required
- Open source software
- No data is sent to external services
