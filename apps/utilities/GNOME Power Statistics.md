# GNOME Power Statistics

**Category:** Utilities → Power Management<br><br>
**Website:** [https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gnome-power-manager/](https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gnome-power-manager/)

Use this app to observe power management on your laptop. GNOME Power Statistics shows historical and current battery information and identifies programs that wake up and use power, which is useful for troubleshooting battery issues or determining what programs are using significant amounts of power.

## Key Features
- View historical and current battery information
- Display graphs of power history, including charge and discharge rates
- Show how power usage changes with processor-intensive tasks or when the system is idle
- Identify programs that wake up and use power
- Troubleshoot laptop battery issues

## How to Get It
GNOME Power Statistics is available in the Zorin OS Software app and can also be downloaded from the official website.

[Download GNOME Power Statistics](https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gnome-power-manager/)
