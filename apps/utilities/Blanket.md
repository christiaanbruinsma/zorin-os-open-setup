# Blanket

**Category:** Utilities → Ambient Sounds<br>
**Website:** [https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket)

Use this app to listen to different sounds. Blanket helps you improve focus and increase productivity, or fall asleep in a noisy environment.

## Key Features
- Play various ambient sounds to aid focus and productivity
- Useful for sleeping in noisy environments
- Simple and intuitive interface
- Part of GNOME Circle

## How to Get It
Blanket is available in the Zorin OS Software app and can also be installed from Flathub.

[Get Blanket on Flathub](https://flathub.org/apps/details/com.rafaelmardojai.Blanket)
