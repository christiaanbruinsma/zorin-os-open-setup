# Camera

**Category:** Utilities → Camera<br><br>
**Website:** [https://apps.gnome.org/Snapshot/](https://apps.gnome.org/Snapshot/)

Use this app to take pictures and videos on your computer, tablet, or phone.

## Key Features
- Capture photos using your webcam
- Record videos with your device's camera
- Simple and intuitive interface
- Gallery viewer for your captures

## How to Get It
Camera comes installed by default on Zorin OS. It is also available in the Zorin OS Software app and can be downloaded from the official website.

[Get Camera](https://apps.gnome.org/Snapshot/)
