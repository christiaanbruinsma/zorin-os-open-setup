# Obsidian

**Category:** Productivity → Note Taking<br>
**Website:** [https://obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md)

Use this app as a free and flexible tool for your private thoughts. Obsidian stores notes locally on your device as plain text Markdown files, giving you full ownership of your data.

## Key Features
- Stores notes privately on your device for offline access
- Uses open file formats (Markdown) to prevent vendor lock-in
- Thousands of plugins and themes for customization
- Create links between notes to build a personal knowledge base
- Graph view to visualize relationships between your notes
- Canvas for brainstorming, diagramming, and organizing ideas
- Optional end-to-end encrypted Sync for accessing notes across devices
- Version history for tracking changes to your notes

## How to Get It
Obsidian is available in the Zorin OS Software app and can also be downloaded from the official website.

[Download Obsidian](https://obsidian.md)
