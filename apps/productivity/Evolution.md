# Evolution

**Category:** Productivity → Email<br><br>
**Website:** [https://wiki.gnome.org/Apps/Evolution](https://wiki.gnome.org/Apps/Evolution)

Use this app to manage your email, calendar, and contacts in one place. Evolution is a full-featured personal information manager with a clean interface and supports Outlook, Gmail, and IMAP accounts.

## Key Features
- Full email client with support for multiple accounts and protocols (including IMAP, POP3)
- Integrated calendar and address book
- Supports encryption (S/MIME, PGP)

## How to Get It
Evolution is available in the Zorin OS Software app and can also be downloaded from the official website.

[Download Evolution](https://wiki.gnome.org/Apps/Evolution)

## Privacy & Notes
- Open-source application
- Stores data locally by default
- Supports encrypted email communication
