# Signal Desktop

**Category:** Productivity → Communication<br>
**Website:** [https://signal.org/](https://signal.org/)

Use this app to communicate with end-to-end encryption. Signal Desktop allows you to send messages, make voice and video calls, share files, and more with state-of-the-art encryption that keeps your conversations secure and private.

## Key Features
- State-of-the-art end-to-end encryption for all communications
- Send text, voice messages, photos, videos, GIFs, and files
- Make crystal-clear voice and video calls
- Group chats for staying connected with family, friends, and coworkers
- Encrypted stickers
- No ads, no trackers, no affiliate marketers
- Free to use with no long-distance charges

## How to Get It
Signal Desktop is available in the Zorin OS Software app and can also be downloaded from the official website.

[Download Signal Desktop](https://signal.org/download/linux/)

## Privacy & Notes
- Open-source application
- Developed by Signal Foundation (Signal Messenger LLC)
- Independent nonprofit organization
- Privacy-focused by design
- Uses the Signal Protocol for encryption
