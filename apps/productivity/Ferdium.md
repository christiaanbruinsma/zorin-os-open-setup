# Ferdium

**Category:** Productivity → Communication
**Website:** [https://ferdium.org/](https://ferdium.org/)

Use this app to have all your services in one place. Ferdium is a community-built tool that lets you add and manage multiple services from a single interface.

## Key Features
- Add all your services in one place for quick and easy access
- Workspaces to separate work and private time
- Built-in Todo panel
- Support for custom services
- Anonymous access without requiring an account (data stays on your device)
- Cloud sync for services and workspaces across devices
- Resource saving by hibernating inactive services
- Privacy features to control notification content

## How to Get It
Ferdium can be downloaded from the official website.

[Download Ferdium](https://ferdium.org/)

## Privacy & Notes
- Open-source application
- Developed by Ferdium Team
