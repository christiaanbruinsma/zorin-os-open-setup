# Thunderbird

**Category:** Productivity → Email
**Website:** [https://www.thunderbird.net/](https://www.thunderbird.net/)

Use this app as a powerful email, calendar, and contacts application. Thunderbird provides a fast, unified way to access all your messages, calendars, and contacts, allowing you to filter and organize emails the way you like, with support for managing all accounts separately or in a unified inbox.

## Key Features
- Unified inbox for multiple email accounts
- Integrated calendar for managing events and tasks
- Contacts management
- Tag feature for organizing and quickly finding emails
- Customizable filters and organization options
- Extensible with add-ons
- Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux
- Supports multiple email providers

## How to Get It
Thunderbird is available in the Zorin OS Software app and can also be downloaded from the official website.

[Download Thunderbird](https://www.thunderbird.net/)
